# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-27

Drawing refinements by Marcio Arêde.

- m: rebuilt as the n arch repeated twice, with the same notch where the arch leaves the stem and without the steps along the x-height. Width and spacing unchanged.
- f: the hook curve now runs into the stem, removing the corner at the joint.
- A, M, N, V, W: the joints of the diagonals are as thick as the horizontal bars (apex of A; top and base of M and N; point of V; top and base of W). Stroke angles, widths and spacing unchanged.
- Accented and composed forms of these letters follow automatically.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-27

First public release.

- Regular (600) in OTF, TTF and WOFF2.
- Lowercase core redesigned from the arede wordmark: a, r, e, d taken from the logo; b, c, f, h, i, m, n, o, p, q, u derived from them; k, l, j adjusted to the new ascender height.
- Stylistic set ss06: n with both corners rounded.
- All other glyphs inherited from Space Grotesk 2.0.0.
